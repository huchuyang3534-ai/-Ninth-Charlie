# Data and Figure Reuse Terms

Author-generated processed result tables, compact benchmark outputs, and figures in this
repository may be reused for research and review purposes with attribution to the
manuscript:

N. Hu, C. Hu, J. Sun, and S. Li, "Acoustic-Guided Generative Assimilation for Estimating
Ocean-Interior Structure From Surface and Acoustic Observations."

The repository does not change the terms of any third-party source data. Raw or upstream
HYCOM, Argo, SWellEx-96, ACOBAR, and Kuroshio Extension survey data remain subject to the
licenses, citation requirements, and access conditions of their original providers.

Files under `code/` are covered by the MIT License in `LICENSE`. Manuscript text and
third-party bibliographic metadata are provided for scholarly review and citation of the
submitted paper.
